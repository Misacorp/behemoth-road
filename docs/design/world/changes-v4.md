# Changes in world bible v4

What `world-v4.md` did with Misa's direction, with `premise-options.md`, and with v3. A = adopted, Ad = adapted, D = declined (with a reason).

## Misa's direction

| Point                                                                                                                                                                              | Done                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Creatures are creatures, not terrain; the world acknowledges them; no mechanics required                                                                                           | A. Nobody becomes a hill or land. Creatures show up in peoples' customs, place names, the calendar, settlement signs and far-off silhouettes. Mechanics stay optional (havoc, boon and personality tags). |
| Huge, personalities, havoc, boons for good tending                                                                                                                                 | A. Each of the 7 kinds has a personality, its havoc and its boon (section 3).                                                                                                                             |
| Peoples relate differently; some harness, some destroy, some help                                                                                                                  | A. A triangle: the Wheelhouse harnesses, the Tally ends them, the Open Gate helps. Each people also reads the same five facts its own way (2).                                                            |
| An unknown, practically unlimited number; rare but known to everyone                                                                                                               | A. Narrator fact 1 ("Nobody has counted them"); far creatures on the map's edge (5.3).                                                                                                                    |
| Seed (a): new, sent or born, bound for a place that needs it                                                                                                                       | A, as **young** creatures that stay. The origin differs per people: born, sent, gifted, built, cursed.                                                                                                    |
| Seed (b): old, escorted out of the world, with last tasks                                                                                                                          | A, as **old** creatures that go on. The tasks happen at leg ends.                                                                                                                                         |
| Combine or choose                                                                                                                                                                  | Combined. One premise, two shapes, marked on the veiled card. Young kinds come first.                                                                                                                     |
| Magma serpent melting a mountain                                                                                                                                                   | A. The Cinder Serpent; its meltwater floods are the Tally's oldest grievance.                                                                                                                             |
| Party: Open Gate plus "hear the call"                                                                                                                                              | A. A company under an old law whose members say they hear calls. The narrator never confirms it (question 5).                                                                                             |
| No noun for the journey; "the trail" only as the map mark; no "road"                                                                                                               | A. "The going" from premise-options is declined too, because it is still a noun. Roadbook becomes the Gatebook; the Caravan "never sells the way back".                                                   |
| Stay or go on                                                                                                                                                                      | A, both: young ones stay, old ones go on, and riders may go with them (question 2).                                                                                                                       |
| Design for multi-leg journeys                                                                                                                                                      | A. `Leg[]` with a share, a turn and a task. Today one leg; nothing changes while there is one.                                                                                                            |
| Next creature tied to the party's story                                                                                                                                            | A. Two pens and two calls in the Quiet; the Gate can open one.                                                                                                                                            |
| Already decided (no digest, ending lines, madness parked, rain, lore after loss, wipe, rosters, recruits and veterans, vows, veiled pick, terrain names, tow line, absent players) | A. All listed in section 0 and followed. The digest is removed throughout. Recruits join the Gate, and vows are made at the gate.                                                                         |
