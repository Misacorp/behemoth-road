# Review of world v4: a player's read

A friend of Misa's: plays cosy-but-dangerous co-op on my phone with a Discord group and loves Ghibli. I'm coming to this cold.

## 1. Do I care about the creature?

Yes.

- **Mossback.** Instant yes. "Curious, slow, easily delighted, follows its nose" is a Ghibli forest spirit. Eating someone's thatch is the right trouble: cute, but someone is cold tonight because of us.
- **Bellmother.** My favourite. Nearly blind, wants quiet, walks through walls when scared, lets three flocks go before she leaves. That's a whole film.
- **Cloudwhale.** "Nobody looks up for long; everyone looks up." Lovely.
- **Cinder Serpent.** Good because it hurts people (the oldest Tally knots are serpent knots). Escorting something warm that you know burned villages is a feeling I haven't had in a game before.
- **Thornstag, Reedwyrm, Rimeox** feel thinner. "Proud", "playful, vain" and "patient, stubborn" are fine as words, but nothing yet makes me picture them. Rimeox's "slow to trust, loyal after" is a great arc if I could _see_ it change over a season.

## 2. Why am I helping? Do I like the Open Gate?

Mostly clear, and yes, I like it. "Where a creature walks, no gate stays shut" is a good law. A group from every people who claim to hear a call that nobody can prove is a perfect excuse for a Discord group of mismatched characters. Never confirming the call is right. Keep it.

What I didn't get at first: **we're springing creatures out of pens.** That's the hook, and it's buried. The Wheelhouse has the Mossback in a pen and we open it. That's a jailbreak, and it explains why the captors come after us. Say it up front.

The other thing: my character leaves play when the season ends. That's beautiful and a bit scary. I'd accept it only if the ending says something about _us_, not just the creature.

## 3. What confuses me, or is too many names

- **Season one setting-out.** There are two lines. The first has frost melting off the fence in a line, which reads like the Cinder Serpent, not a moss creature. Who is Haldo? Which line do I actually see?
- **Kelith and Wrena** show up in the samples with no introduction. Are they players? NPCs? Wrena Long-step?
- If creatures appear "far from anywhere, already grown", how did the Mossback get into a pen?
- **Names, all at once:** Wheelhouse, Brightsmiths, Tally, Open Gate, Glass Caravan, Reedchoir, Mothfolk, hill folk, river folk, Larun, Brackenlow, Gatebook, Hook Line, Stirring, Wander, Turning, Quiet, Greyfold, Bellwater. Too many to read in one go. The season one cut (eight names) is the right call. Later on, let me meet peoples through their signs (bell, pearl, lantern, knot) before their names.
- Brightsmiths vs Glass Caravan vs Wheelhouse: three groups tied to the same old metal. I lost track.

## 4. Endings

**Make me happy:**

- Mossback safe. "The hill folk are arguing about a new name for it" made me grin.
- Cinder Serpent safe.

**Sting in a good way:**

- Cloudwhale: "fewer of you at the fire." Best line in the doc.
- Mossback wounded: "the valley greens anyway, slowly, from that circle out". Sad and hopeful at once.
- Bellmother: the gulls not moving is perfect.
- Tally loss: "sits a long time without tying the next". A villain who grieves. Great.

**Fall flat:**

- Thornstag wounded. "Nobody tries to stop it" doesn't land; I don't know what I'm meant to feel.
- Pursuer loss. "The bakers light their ovens early" can read as _they're cooking it_. If that's on purpose, it's grim. If not, change it.
- Hunger loss is fine but bleak, with no human detail to hold onto.

## 5. Top 5 suggestions

1. **Make the jailbreak the opening.** Use one setting-out line for season one: clearly the Mossback, clearly a Wheelhouse pen, a bit of a heist. Fix the frost line.
2. **Put the party in every ending.** One clause about who stays or who rides on, since our characters leave play. Even "someone builds a hut on the rim" would do.
3. **Give each kind one visible delight in play.** It doesn't need the body. A Mossback detour to an orchard, Rimeox finally walking close to the fire. That's how I'd fall for the thinner kinds.
4. **Introduce names slowly, and explain the stray ones.** Remove Haldo, Kelith and Wrena from the samples or say who they are. Fold the Brightsmiths into one sentence about old metal.
5. **Give the Wheelhouse one human beat in season one,** like a miller's kid watching the pen open, so the captors aren't just hooks. The Tally gets real grief; the Wheelhouse deserves a face too.
