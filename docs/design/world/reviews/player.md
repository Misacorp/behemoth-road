# World v2, read by a player

I skipped the code and data and read what I'd actually see.

## 1. First impressions

**What excites me.** The creature is the weather forecast. It lies down early before rain and turns its back to the wind. Totoro-level charm, and a reason to look at it daily. The garlands too: a creature covered in flowers means a good season, and a bare one means trouble, so I can tell how we're doing in one glance. A little shrine where a friend's character was lost. Old cairns with a friend's name from last season. The endings: "It is still a hill" got me.

**What confuses me.** There are a lot of names. The game says behemoth, the world says walker, ours is a Mossback, and each people has its own word for them (Grandfolk, Ulle's children, bearers, lodes, draught, the bound). Then there are Brightsmiths, Harrowmen, the Wheelhouse, the Glass Caravan, the Reedchoir, Mothfolk and the Under-One. I'd have no idea who is who for the first week. "Ninth of the Homing" sounds lovely, but it doesn't tell me how far along we are. Is that early? Are we nearly there?

**What I'd skip.** The festival backstories and the lore about who made the collars, unless something on the map pulled me to them. I'd read the fragments only once someone in chat said "wait, read this".

## 2. Would the digests and cards get me to open the app?

The digests that work give me a reason to tap:

- **#4** "Faint ringing from the reeds; the Mossback's ears are turned that way." Something's coming and I want to know what.
- **#2** "Smoke behind, closer than yesterday." That's real tension in one line.
- **#3** "the Roadbook has a new page. Something glints on the far bank." A reward and a lure together.
- **#6** "Everyone came through the night." Lovely to read after a close one.

The ones that don't:

- **#1** "Wind from the north… Kai's Scout reached the river first." It's weather and a race result. I'd scroll past.
- **#5**: "Thirty-second of the Homing" is a mouthful to open with.

Forty posts a season is a lot. If quiet days read like #1, I'd mute the channel by week two. The teaser line is the hook, so every post should have one, or quiet days should be a single line.

The story cards are better than the digests. My favourites:

- "watching the far hills as if one of them might move"
- "One of them is moving"
- the heel-shaped meadow
- the child called inside, which made me feel guilty in a good way
- Wren's cairn with "someone has added a fifth since"

One that doesn't work: the rain card's barking dog. I'd go looking for a dog that doesn't exist.

## 3. Too much text, or too obscure?

The cards are a good length: one to three lines fit a phone fine. These are the problems:

- **Names.** In anything players see, I'd use "walker" and "the Wheelhouse" and not much else in the first season. Bring in the other peoples' words through sayings later.
- **Dates.** "Thirty-ninth of the Homing" needs a number or the route strip beside it.
- **How events are solved.** For the Hook Line, I'd never work out the decoy stake, the grappling-hook double strike and the mislead boon without being told. Those need very clear visual hints, or our group just fails them and doesn't know why.
- Words like "window", "reach" and "word" must never show up in what players see.

## 4. What I'd screenshot

- The creature covered in garlands late in the route.
- The ending plate: the new hill with soft moss, or the crooked one.
- A friend's shrine, or their old cairn turning up next season.
- The Passing: "the hills are moving", and the plate showing next season's creature. I'd post that with "WHAT".
- The lantern line on the hill during the Owing.
- The Hook Line chain lying slack, with "Someone back there is going to be shouted at."
- A private card ("the Keeper learned something"). That's the one that starts chat, and I'd tease it before showing it.

## 5. What I'd want more of

- **The creature's personality.** Small habits, favourites, how it reacts to each of us. It's the heart of the game, and it gets fewer lines than the Wheelhouse.
- **Small jokes.** The Caravan selling the pearls back, and the guy getting shouted at. A little more of that.
- **Things to do at festivals.** Let us carry a pot up the hill, not just watch the lanterns.
- **Our names in the legend.** The "previously" openers are the best idea here. I want them to name us.

## 6. Top 6 suggestions

1. **Lead every digest with the creature, not the weather.** "The Mossback lay down early" beats "Wind from the north".
2. **Every digest ends with a teaser,** and quiet days get one short line. Otherwise people mute the channel.
3. **Cut the names down for the first season.** One word for walkers, two peoples you meet, and the rest arrive through sayings and fragments.
4. **Show how far along we are next to the calendar date,** with the route strip or "day 9 of 40". The pretty date alone doesn't tell me.
5. **Make losses and memories name friends:** shrines, cairns, garlands credited to whoever earned them, and legends that mention us. That's what we'd talk about in Discord.
6. **Give each event's solutions something you can see on the map.** If our group can't read the Hook Line or the Reedchoir from the map, we'll just think the game is unfair. Keep details in lines tied to things that actually exist (no phantom dogs).

I'd play this. Cosy, a bit sad, a bit dangerous. Fewer proper nouns, more creature.
