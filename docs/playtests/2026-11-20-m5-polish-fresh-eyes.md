# Fresh-eyes playtest: Milestone 5 polish (2026-11-20)

A second agent that had never seen the project played `m5/integration` (b92f349, after the playtest polish round) locally as three players: a scout on a phone (390×844, touch), an engineer and an envoy on desktop (1440×900), with the short dev season and "next day". It played one season, lost on day 10 at about 70 % of the route, and ran out of budget before a second season. No page errors.

## The first playtest's issues, checked

- **Phone targeting:** moving to neighbours worked every time; no single tap spent actions (every action armed, then confirmed). A tap on your own empty tile selects the character (survey marks on neighbours toggle) rather than opening a card, consistently. On desktop the own-tile card on a settlement sat below the tile, clear of the tiles above. The creature's rider card still covers the tiles just north of the footprint.
- **Out of reach:** a far tile shows its terrain card with a closed eye and no move; the tester did not read it as "too far". Tapping a hazard drops the selection silently.
- **Empty surveys:** five empty surveys; the explored-empty look and open eye read as empty after a moment. Small rocks and grass still could not be told from usable things before stepping on them.
- **Greyed actions:** readable: a greyed action showed the role emblem needed.
- **Stone and iron:** none found in ten days; nothing was built.
- **Settlements:** still tappable after a parley, with favour marks; no boon was reached.
- **Pressure:** early enough: captors on day 2, a rockslide by day 4, swarms by day 8; the season was lost.
- **Ending:** "Lost" looked clean on both sizes. On the phone the game's buttons (next day, join) were still in the page behind it (not tested whether they can be tapped).

## Still confusing

- Which way home is, and what the peril gauge and dashed fading tiles mean.
- Desktop: a tile offers only survey until your own character is selected; a tile with another player opens their card instead of moving there.
- With your character selected, a tap on a survey-marked tile plans a move, not a survey.
- The pack stayed empty all season.

## Highlights

The art and the route strip; move previews with arrow and pips; arm-then-confirm stops mistakes. No explanatory prose, only names and the ending title.

## Follow-up

Passed to the lead; nothing acted on.
