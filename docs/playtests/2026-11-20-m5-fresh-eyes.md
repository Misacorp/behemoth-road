# Fresh-eyes playtest: Milestone 5 (2026-11-20)

An agent that had never seen the project played `m5/web` (74d5c75, with Milestone 4's polish) locally as three players: one on a phone (390×844) and two on desktop (1440×900), with the short dev season and "next day". It learned the game only from the screen. Season 1 ended "Home safe" on day 13; season 2 ended "Lost" on day 12, one step short of home.

## What worked

- Local dev started first time (copy `.dev.vars.example` to `.dev.vars`).
- The ending scenes, safe and lost.
- Danger read without words: the swarm with its strength badge, the pulsing ring before a strike, the darkening vignette, a teammate left behind turning grey.
- The route strip read as "walk it home" on day 1; the loop clicked on day 7 (befriend a settlement, gift of forage, feed, signs clear).
- An envoy's parley shows no risk mark; a scout's does.
- No explanatory prose; only names and the ending titles.

## What confused or broke

- Phone: a popover on your own tile covers the tiles to the north, so a tap meant for a tile can hit a popover button (a fire was lit by accident). Tapping your own tile is unreliable (the first tap pans). A tap on a tile out of reach opens the inspect card silently.
- Season 1 had no peril until day 13: too calm.
- Surveys found nothing visible in about ten tries; pebble and stone decorations look like loot; no stone or iron was found, so no unlock could be built.
- A greyed site action does not show which role it needs; cliffs refuse entry with no feedback.
- A settlement whose boon is taken gives no popover; mislead and shelter showed no visible effect.
- The ending's summary bar sits over the characters.
- The "your character" and "behemoth" buttons only re-centre; the tester expected a sheet.

## Top suggestions (the tester's order)

1. Fix tile targeting on the phone (popover placement, one-tap own tile).
2. Make surveys pay off or mark an empty hex; stop decorations looking like loot.
3. Show which role a greyed action needs; make stone and iron findable.
4. Bring pressure in earlier (first hunger sign or threat by day 3).
5. Show what a boon does (a mislead arrow on the threat), and keep a used settlement tappable.
